How it works

From interview to sequence, before the coffee's cold.

Ask for the beats. Pull the selects. Send them straight to your timeline in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve.

The problem

The story is hours deep in the rushes.

A normal transcript gives you the words, but not the context, the hesitation, the contradiction or the moment that makes a scene work. So the scrubbing, logging and re-listening still fall to you.

What Smithy does

Reads your footage with an editor's eye.

It follows ideas across interviews, hears what sits between the lines, and finds the beats, quotes and selects where the emotion carries the story.

What you get

Less searching. More shaping the cut.

Go from rushes to a story you can trace, check and stand behind, in the edit, in review and on air.

Transcripts

Transcripts are dead text. Smithy brings them to life.

Drop in your rushes. Every word, speaker and timecode, across every file. Highlight a line to keep it, or ask Smithy where else it comes up.
